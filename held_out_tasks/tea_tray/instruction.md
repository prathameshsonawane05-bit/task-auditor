# The sugar bowl on the tea tray

Sweet tea, in this hall, means twice the spoons the visitor asked for. Plain tea means you put down the spoon and leave the number alone. Zero spoons stays zero either way. A negative spoon is not a thing we serve, so the bowl only has to answer for zero and the ordinary counts.

`sugar_spoons(kind, spoons)` hears two words. "sweet" doubles. "plain" returns the same count. Anything else, including a capital letter that does not match, is a different drink. Raise ValueError and say "tea is sweet or plain".
