# The pencil mark on the tea urn

The urn is slow and nobody believes it until they have stood there. One mug needs 120 seconds. Two mugs need 240. Three need 360. It really is just the mug count times two minutes, and I am writing it down so the next person does not boil a single mug and call that a round.

`kettle_seconds(mugs)` is that mark. Zero mugs, or a negative count, is not a round. Raise ValueError and say "brew at least one mug".
