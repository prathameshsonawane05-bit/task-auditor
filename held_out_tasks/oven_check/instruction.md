# The tape on the bake-sale oven

Mina put a strip of tape on the oven after the scones went pale and then, the week after, nearly black. The window she trusts is 180 degrees through 200 degrees. Both of those numbers are still good. 179 is too cool. 201 is too hot.

`oven_ready(temp)` answers that and only that. True means the trays can go in. False means wait, either way. She did not ask for a message. A number outside the window is a false, not an argument.
