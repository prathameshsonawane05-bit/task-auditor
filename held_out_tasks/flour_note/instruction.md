# The margin of the scone card

The old card says a cup of flour is 120 grams. Lena does not want a debate about scooped cups versus level cups on a Saturday. One cup is 120. Three cups are 360. Zero cups is a bowl she has not started, and that is zero grams.

`flour_grams(cups)` is that margin and no more. A negative cup count is a smudge. Raise ValueError and say "a cup count cannot be negative".
