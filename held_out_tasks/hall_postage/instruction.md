# The card by the parcel scale

Nia pinned this above the Saturday scale because the new volunteers kept guessing out loud and charging whatever felt kind.

`postage_pence(grams)` reads that card and nothing cleverer. A parcel under 100 grams, including an empty box that weighs nothing, is 80 pence. From 100 grams up to but not including 500 grams, the card says 150 pence. From 500 grams through 2000 grams, it says 320 pence. One hundred is already the middle price. Five hundred is already the heavy price. Two thousand is still the heavy price, the last box that fits in the bike basket.

A negative number means the scale was misread. Raise ValueError and say "the scale cannot read a negative". Anything past 2000 grams does not fit the bike. Raise ValueError and say "too heavy for the bike".
